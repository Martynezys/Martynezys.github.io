P-01 · Featured · Planned thesis topicIn progress

Managed power distribution board for an SBC home server

One 12 V / 150 W supply feeds a custom PCB. An ESP32-S3 on the board switches every port remotely, measures voltage, current and power per port with INA226 sensors, and protects each port with overcurrent cut-off and soft-start.

My first soft-start design turned out, in simulation, to never switch the FET on. The fix cut simulated inrush into 470 µF from 129 A to 1.08 A.

12 V · 150 W · 2×12 V + 2×5 V SBC ports · INA226 · ESP32-S3 · Altium · LTspice

About

I'm a second-year Applied Electronics student at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, VŠB – Technical University of Ostrava, originally from Žilina, Slovakia. I passed my first year with all 60 credits.

I learn best by building. Right now that's a power distribution and monitoring board for my own home server, which is also turning into my bachelor's thesis. I'm most drawn to measurement, embedded systems and signals, and I'm teaching myself digital design on an FPGA on the side.

I document projects like real engineering work: decision records, a dated lab notebook, simulations and measurements, including the parts that didn't work.
